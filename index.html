I'm a Multimedia Engineer, graduated from the University of Valencia, currently working as a Junior Back-End Developer at RELP — a renewable energy organisation operating across the whole world.

My background sits at the intersection of engineering and creative technology. I care about building things that are well-structured, maintainable, and genuinely useful — whether that's an API, an internal tool, or a web platform.

I spent a year in Bergen, Norway on an Erasmus exchange, studying master-level subjects including Machine Learning, Software Security, Web Science, Data Visualization, and Human-Computer Interaction — which broadened my perspective considerably.

I adapt quickly, communicate clearly in Spanish and English, and enjoy working in environments where I can keep learning. I'm an EU citizen relocating to Bergen, Norway this October.
